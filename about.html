Forty-three years of one trade, done properly.

Electro Enterprise has manufactured electrical accessories in Ahmedabad under the ELECTRO mark since 1979 — modular plates and holders, concealed and surface boxes, distribution boards, wiring accessories, power strips and LED foot lights.

The company

Electro Enterprise was founded in Ahmedabad, Gujarat in 1979 by V. N. Gajera, and trades as ELECTRO. The business supplies dealers and distributors — it is a trade manufacturer, not a retailer.

The range is deliberately built as a system rather than a collection. Holders, plates and boxes share the same base footprints — 3.5×3.5, 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7 — so a single job can be standardised end to end, and finishes can be mixed on the same wall run without changing plate systems. That is why the catalogue reaches 445 codes across only 35 families: depth in sizes and finishes rather than a scatter of unrelated products.

Quality is held under an ISO 9001:2015 system, and every code in the catalogue is manufactured in India.

The series

Seven series divide the range by format and finish rather than by price alone: Venox for the modular holder and concealed-box system, Star for the complete switch-plate line in white, glossy and matt, Rio for the broadest holder finish spread, Eco for cost-sensitive jobs, Euro for general accessories and plug tops, Nova for portable flex boards, and Fotolia for LED foot lights and night lamps.
