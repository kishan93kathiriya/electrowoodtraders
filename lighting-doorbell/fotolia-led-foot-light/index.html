Fotolia LED Foot Light

Decorative LED foot lights and night lamps in scenery, diamond, western, stair, and shutter faceplate designs, sized from a single-module indicator light up to a large 8x5 panel. Built for skirting-level or stairway installation to light walkways at night without a full room light, with matt black and matt grey options on the western design, plus sensor and plain night-lamp variants that plug straight into a 2-pin socket rather than mounting in a wall.
