Wire Circle Cable Clip

Circular wire clips from 4mm to 25mm in white and black, plus double-nail clips and oval or flat clip formats, for fixing a single cable or wire bundle directly to a wall or ceiling surface. Clip diameter should match the cable gauge — smaller clips for lighting circuits, larger for heavier power runs.
