Venox series

New Generation Modular Holder

The Venox modular base system pairs a single ring or base plate with interchangeable holders — angle holders, batten holders, ceiling roses, and blank or hole dummy plates — so one plate footprint (3.5x3.5, 5x5, 6x6, or 7x7) adapts to whatever fitting a room needs, including LED panel and junction lights. Holders use heavy brass fittings for a secure, lasting connection, finished for a clean, elegant look at an affordable price point. A practical base range for electricians who want to standardise fittings across a job without stocking five different plate systems.
