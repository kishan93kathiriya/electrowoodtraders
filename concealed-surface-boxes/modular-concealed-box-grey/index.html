Venox series

Modular Concealed Box — Grey

PVC concealed (in-wall) boxes in grey, from a half-module box up to an 18-module box, plus dedicated fan boxes and a compact 3.5x3.5 box. These mount inside the wall before plastering, giving switches and sockets a secure, standard mounting point behind the visible plate. The grey finish matches the Venox grey holder and plate ranges for a consistent colour scheme even where the box itself won't usually be seen.
