Concealed boxes mount inside the wall before plastering; surface boxes fit directly onto the wall face for retrofit and exposed-conduit work. PVC boxes run from a half-module up to 18 module in grey, orange and black, with dedicated fan boxes. Where a job calls for a sturdier back box, the metal range offers Zink Plus Heavy (1 mm, 20 gauge), GI Medium (0.8 mm, 22 gauge) and GI Low (0.6 mm, 24 gauge).
