01 / The range

Six categories. One system that fits together.

Every plate footprint, box and holder in the catalogue is built to the same module sizes, so a single job can be standardised end to end — from the concealed box in the wall to the finish plate on it.

01

Modular Switches & Plates

Venox, Star, Rio and Eco holders, ring and base plates from 3.5×3.5 to 7×7, plus Silverline and Blackline modular plates in white, black, grey and matt finishes. 15 families · 194 codes 02

Concealed & Surface Boxes

PVC concealed boxes ½ to 18 module in grey, orange and black; metal boxes in three gauges; Whiteline and Silverline surface gang boxes. 8 families · 93 codes 03

Wiring Accessories

Cable ties 100–450 mm, wire circle and double-nail clips, Euro plug tops and connectors, drywall screws. 4 families · 75 codes 04

Distribution & MCB Boxes

Arc single and double-door SPN boards to 16 way, PVC glass-front MCB boxes, three-phase TPN boxes. 3 families · 34 codes 05

Power Strips & Regulators

Ora and Classic extension boards 1.5–5 m, the Nova flex-board range, 4-PF fan regulators in six design lines. 3 families · 28 codes 06

LED Foot Lights & Door Bells

Fotolia foot lights and night lamps in scenery, diamond, western, stair and shutter faces — from a 1-module indicator to an 8×5 panel — plus five door bell tones. 2 families · 21 codes

04 / Why ELECTRO

Four promises printed on every catalogue.

The same four claims have led the ELECTRO catalogue for years, because they are what electricians and dealers actually judge the parts on.

01

Easy installation

One plate footprint per size across the range, so holders, plates and boxes interchange without restocking a second system.

02

Robust design

Heavy brass fittings in the holder range, PVC and metal box options in three gauges, sized to the wall and the code.

03

Long life

Finishes specified for the job — white, glossy black, matt black, matt grey and silverline — held to the same tooling for years.

04

Eco friendly

Manufactured in Ahmedabad under an ISO 9001:2015 quality system, with material and packing standardised across the range.
